The practice

Every plan begins with a functional assessment and a goal that genuinely matters to you, whether that is walking the pier again, returning to the pitch, or lifting your child without pain.

Treatment combines manual therapy, graded exercise prescription and clear patient education, documented to the standards CORU requires. My MSc in Health Informatics shapes how progress is tracked: structured outcome measures, reviewed session by session, so that we both know whether the plan is working.

01 Assess

A full history, functional movement screening and objective baselines, all within the first session.

02 Treat

Joint mobilisation, dry needling, therapeutic taping, myofascial release and vestibular protocols where they are indicated.

03 Progress

A home programme designed around your week, monitored outcomes, and a clear discharge plan you leave with.

Selected projects

2021 to 2022 · Hospital rotation

A rotational internship across five specialities

I completed structured clinical rotations in paediatrics, neurology, orthopaedics, geriatrics and cardiothoracic care at Index Medical College and Research Centre, delivering both individual and group rehabilitation programmes.

The role also covered paediatric functional mobility training and community intervention programmes, including home visits and outreach services.

2025 · Sports performance

Strength, conditioning and injury prevention for UL Women's Gaelic Football

I designed and delivered strength and conditioning sessions built around the specific performance demands of Gaelic football.

Alongside that, I put injury prevention strategies and safe return-to-play protocols in place, adapting each programme as athlete performance and recovery data changed.
